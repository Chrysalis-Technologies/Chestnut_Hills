This is the public small model, NOT the full Seek or iNaturalist identification model.
It has 507 outputs. This app offers 174 flowering plants (including some trees and
shrubs) and 18 mushroom/fungus taxa. Three lichen taxa and other groups are not
offered in these two modes. See Supported species for the exact coverage.
No geographic range model or regional filter is used. GPS is saved with observations
when requested; it does not affect model scores. No data is sent to iNaturalist.

The separate nature image path follows the upstream Android implementation:
https://github.com/inaturalist/vision-camera-plugin-inatvision/blob/version-7/android/src/main/java/com/visioncameraplugininatvision/ImageClassifier.java
Center-square crop, Android bilinear resize to 299x299, x-major RGB float 0..255.
The model already outputs normalized scores; no second softmax or per-category
renormalization is applied. Suggestions require the overall top class to belong to
the selected category and score at least 0.25; up to five matches scoring 0.10 or
more are shown. These thresholds are heuristics, not measured probabilities.

Mushroom photo suggestions can be wrong. This app cannot determine edibility.
Never eat a mushroom or use a plant based on these suggestions. Independent expert
identification is needed for decisions about consumption.
