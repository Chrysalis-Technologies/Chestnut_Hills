Audio input: mono, 48000 Hz, 144000 float32 samples per three-second window,
range [-1, 1]. Model output: 6522 logits, converted once with sigmoid.
Four non-overlapping windows per twelve-second recording; suggestions show
the highest score for each species across the windows, threshold 0.25, top five.
These scores are not calibrated probabilities. There is no regional range filter.
Some other animal species are included in the upstream model. The 11 non-event
classes listed in the BirdNET FAQ are excluded from identification choices.
https://birdnet-team.github.io/BirdNET-Analyzer/stable/faq.html
