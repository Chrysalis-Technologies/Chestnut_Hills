New York catalog
Source: iNaturalist public aggregate research-grade species counts, place 48
(New York, US), retrieved 2026-11-03. Exact scientific-name matches only.
https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations/species_counts?place_id=48&iconic_taxa=Plantae&quality_grade=research
https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations/species_counts?place_id=48&iconic_taxa=Fungi&quality_grade=research
Only names, taxon IDs, ancestor IDs and state-level counts were used. No observation
coordinates, contributor details or source photos are bundled in the NY snapshot.
The app includes 808 plant taxa (806 with NY records), including 14 ferns and allies
and 28 vines, plus 900 mushroom/fungus taxa with NY records. Fern/vine counts overlap
Plants. Old supported labels remain selectable for compatibility. Lichens are not
offered as mushrooms. The list is not a complete NY flora/fungi checklist.
An observation record does not establish nativity, commonness, current nearby
presence or identification accuracy. Some taxa have only one or two NY records.

Scores and limitations
All model outputs compete before category/NY filtering; scores are never
renormalized over the displayed subset. Iris uses its published top >= 0.70 and
top-to-second gap >= 0.25 rule. Fungi uses an uncalibrated heuristic top >= 0.50
and gap >= 0.20. These are model scores, not measured probabilities of correctness.
When the primary model is uncertain, the unchanged iNaturalist Small 2 model may
provide a suggestion using its existing thresholds. Scores are not blended; the
model used is displayed and saved with each observation. A strong global winner
outside the selected category/catalog produces Unknown, not a forced local guess.
Accuracy on New York field photos has not been measured. Ferns and allies include
horsetails and clubmosses. Unsupported species can still be misidentified.
Never eat a mushroom based on this app. Photo suggestions cannot determine edibility.

Original tree, BirdNET and iNaturalist Small 2 weights and licenses are unchanged.
