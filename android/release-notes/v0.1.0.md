First test build of **NY Tree ID**: identify New York State trees from a photo, completely offline.

**What's in it**
- Identifies **99 trees** common in New York, plus a "not a tree in this guide" answer for other plants
- Shows the top matches with confidence, what to check to confirm, and common look-alikes
- Searchable field guide for all 99 trees
- No internet permission: photos never leave the phone

**Accuracy** (7,278 test photos the model never saw)
- Right tree first: **55%**; right tree in the top 3: **74%**
- When the app says "Most likely" (about a third of photos), it's right **92%** of the time
- Best results: fill the square with a single leaf or needle cluster in good light

**Install**
Download `nys-tree-id-v0.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it (Android 8.0+). Android will ask once to allow installs from your browser or Files app.

**Known limitations**
- First build: not yet tested on a real device, so camera framing and speed are unverified
- The model was trained on a CPU; a GPU-trained model should be noticeably more accurate
- Signed with a shared test key, not suitable for an app store

Training photos: iNaturalist contributors (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC), via the iNaturalist Open Dataset. Identifications are suggestions; never eat or use a plant based on this app.
