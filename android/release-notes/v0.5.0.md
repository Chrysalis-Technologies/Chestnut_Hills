# v0.5.0 — Offline flowers and mushrooms

- Choose Trees, Flowers or Mushrooms on the camera screen, then take a photo or choose a local image.
- Bundles iNaturalist's MIT-licensed Small 2 model: 174 flowering plants (including some flowering trees/shrubs) and 18 mushroom/fungus taxa. The supported-species list is available in each result screen.
- Choose a suggested identification or Unknown and save a private photo, notes and optional GPS. Browse, correct and delete observations under Flowers & fungi.
- Flower and mushroom pins join tree and bird pins on the offline sightings map. Missing coordinates produce no pin.
- Mushroom results and saved details explain that photo identification cannot determine edibility. No edible/safe-to-eat labels are provided.
- Existing tree and bird model files, tree preprocessing, tree species list and databases are unchanged. No INTERNET permission or new runtime dependency.

Coverage is limited to the displayed species list. Unsupported species may be misidentified,
and model scores are not measured accuracy. The model has no regional filter. This update
does not add mushroom/flower field-guide illustrations or extend the existing tree-only
CSV/GeoJSON exports. Real-world identification and outdoor GPS testing are still required.
