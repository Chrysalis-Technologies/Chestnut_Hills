# v0.6.0 — More New York plants, ferns, vines and fungi

- Adds **Ferns & allies** and **Vines** camera modes; **Flowers** becomes **Plants**.
- Expands the combined catalog to **808 plant taxa**, including **14 ferns and allies**
  and **28 vines**, plus **900 mushroom/fungus taxa**. The fern and vine groups overlap
  Plants. All except two retained legacy plants have research-grade New York records
  in the bundled November 3, 2026 iNaturalist snapshot.
- Bundles Iris 9 for plants and FungiTastic DINOv3 for fungi. Identification, the
  searchable species list, occurrence counts, credits and licenses work offline.
  The existing Small 2 model remains a fallback for uncertain primary results.
- Save fern/vine photos, notes and optional GPS; browse, correct and delete them,
  and open their colored pins on the existing offline map. Old saved records remain
  usable. Results and saved records retain the model that supplied the suggestion.
- No INTERNET permission, runtime downloads or new runtime dependency. Existing
  tree/BirdNET/Small 2 model files, tree preprocessing and tree species list are unchanged.

NY records indicate reported occurrence, not nativity, abundance, nearby presence
or validated accuracy. Coverage is incomplete. FungiTastic has a Denmark/Europe
training bias; accuracy on New York photos is unmeasured. **Never eat a mushroom
based on this app.** No edible/safe-to-eat labels are provided.

The fungi model is noncommercial under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the DINOv3 terms; Iris
weights/metadata use CC BY 4.0. Full notices and source cards are bundled.
This update does not add new field-guide illustrations or extend tree-only CSV/GeoJSON exports.

New model inference and UI acceptance have **not been run on a device yet**.
Desktop inference smoke tests do not replace Android or outdoor testing. See the
manual checklist in `docs/tasks/nys-nature-expansion.md` before publishing a release.
